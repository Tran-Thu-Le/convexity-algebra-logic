# d1-hierarchy-v2

# Overall Architecture

> **Observe → Axiomatize → Represent**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 2. Structures of Convex Functions

## Goal

Discover and organize the natural structures of convex functions that
will later be abstracted into an algebraic theory.

## Subsections

### 2.1 Objects and Operations

-   Extended class of convex functions
-   Two degenerate elements
-   Three primitive operations
-   Identity elements

### 2.2 Structural Properties

-   Prove the eleven structural properties of the convex model
-   Conclude that these properties motivate the abstract theory
    developed in Section 3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 3. Polar Semiring

## Goal

Axiomatize the structures discovered in Section 2, develop their
intrinsic algebraic theory, and position Polar Semiring among existing
algebraic frameworks.

## Subsections

### 3.1 Axiomatization

-   Definition of Polar Semiring
-   Eleven axioms
-   Independence theorem for the axiom system

### 3.2 Derived Structures

Develop the theory induced from the three primitive operations:

-   Order
-   Join
-   Spator
-   De Morgan laws
-   Monotonicity
-   Distributivity
-   Compatibility
-   Other algebraic theorems

### 3.3 Relation to Residuated Structures

-   Brief introduction to residuated algebras
-   Motivation for comparing Polar Semiring with residual-based
    structures
-   Positioning within the existing algebraic landscape

### 3.4 Impossibility Theorem with Residual

-   Prove that Polar Semiring cannot be represented as a residuated
    structure
-   Establish the boundary between Polar Semiring and residual-based
    algebras

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Section 4. Convex Analysis as a Polar Semiring

## Goal

Show that convex analysis provides a canonical model of Polar Semiring
and identify the classical convex operators as realizations of the
abstract algebra.

## Subsections

### 4.1 Convex Model Theorem

-   Prove that the canonical convex structure satisfies all axioms of
    Polar Semiring

### 4.2 Representation Theorem

-   Represent the derived algebraic structures by classical convex
    operators
-   Derive the corresponding convex-analysis corollaries as immediate
    consequences

------------------------------------------------------------------------
